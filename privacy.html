FilmCap

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

FilmCap is a camera. Your photos, videos and settings stay on your iPhone. FilmCap has no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics of its own. The only information that leaves your iPhone is what is needed to sell and restore a FilmCap Pro membership.

On your iPhone

FilmCap uses these only on your iPhone, and never sends them to us or to anyone else:

  • Camera and microphone, to take photos and record video and sound.
  • Photos, to save what you take into your library and an album named FilmCap, and to show and edit those pictures in the app's darkroom.
  • Location, only if you turn it on in FilmCap's settings, to write where a photo was taken into that photo, as the Camera app does.
  • Your settings and films, including films you import and edit.
  • The free roll: how many photos and videos you have taken, and the films you keep. This is kept in your iPhone's keychain, so it stays if you delete and reinstall the app.

A photo or video leaves your iPhone only when you share it, or when your Photos library syncs it to iCloud under your own iCloud settings.

Purchases

FilmCap Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's Privacy Policy; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

FilmCap uses RevenueCat to check purchases with the App Store and to know which membership you have. For that, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier made by the app on your iPhone (not your name, Apple Account or any advertising identifier);
  • your purchase history in FilmCap: which product, its price and currency, when it was bought or renewed, and the App Store's transaction identifiers;
  • technical details needed to provide the service: app version, iPhone model, iOS version, App Store country and language, and your IP address when the app connects.

This is used to give you the membership you paid for, to restore it on your other devices, and for totals about memberships (for example, how many people subscribe). It is not linked to your identity, not used to track you across apps or websites, and not sold. RevenueCat processes it for us under the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

FilmCap sends no crash or usage reports of its own. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous crash and performance reports, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting

Deleting FilmCap deletes its settings and films from your iPhone; your photos stay in your Photos library until you delete them there. The free roll in the keychain is removed when you erase your iPhone.

RevenueCat keeps purchase records while they are needed for your membership and for accounting. To ask for the records about your purchases to be deleted, or for a copy, contact us (below) with the identifier shown in FilmCap: Settings › Membership › Manage Membership.

Children

FilmCap is not directed to children and collects no information from anyone beyond what is described here.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
