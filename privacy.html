FilmCap

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

FilmCap is a camera. Your photos, videos and settings stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics of our own. The only data that leaves your phone is what is needed to buy or restore a membership.

On your iPhone

These stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Camera and microphone: to take photos and record video and sound.
  • Photos: to save what you take and show it in the darkroom.
  • Location: only if turned on in FilmCap settings, to save where a photo was taken.
  • Settings and films: your app options and any custom films.
  • Free roll count: how many free shots you have used. This stays on your phone so it survives deleting and reinstalling the app.

Photos leave your iPhone only when you share them or your iCloud Photos syncs them.

Purchases

FilmCap Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payments under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details.

FilmCap uses RevenueCat to check purchases. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random ID made by the app, not your name, Apple Account or advertising ID;
  • your purchase history in FilmCap: product, price, currency, dates, and App Store transaction IDs;
  • technical details to run the service: app version, iPhone model, iOS version, App Store country and language, and the internet address the app connects from.

This is used to give you the membership you paid for, restore it on other devices, and count total memberships. It is not used to track you across apps or websites, and never sold. Learn more in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

FilmCap sends no crash or usage reports of its own. Apple may share anonymous crash and performance reports only if you turned on sharing with app developers in iOS Settings.

What the App Store shows

The App Store lists Purchases as data linked to you, because your purchase history connects to that random ID. It is not linked to your name or Apple Account. Everything else is listed as not collected.

Keeping and deleting

Deleting FilmCap removes its settings and films from your iPhone. Photos stay in your Photos library until you delete them there. The free roll count clears when you erase your iPhone.

RevenueCat keeps purchase records while needed for your membership and accounting. To view or delete your purchase records, contact us with the ID shown in FilmCap: Settings › Membership › Manage Membership.

Children

FilmCap is for everyone, children included. It asks no one for personal details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.

Contact

For questions or requests, open an issue, or use the developer contact on FilmCap's App Store page.
