Support

Restore purchases

On a new iPhone or after reinstalling: open FilmCap and tap Settings › Membership › Restore Purchases. Sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

Manage or cancel a subscription

In FilmCap: tap Settings › Membership › Manage Membership. Or in iOS Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Request a refund through Manage Membership in FilmCap, or at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Camera, microphone or Photos access

Open iOS Settings › Apps › FilmCap. Turn on Camera and Microphone, and set Photos to Full Access so the darkroom can show your FilmCap album.

Contact

For anything else, open an issue, or use the developer contact on FilmCap's App Store page.
